How to Scan a Document

  1. Open the Smart Document Scanner.
  2. Tap Scan Document.
  3. Allow camera permission.
  4. Keep the complete document inside the guide.
  5. Tap the white Capture button.
  6. Wait for automatic detection and cleaning.
  7. Tap Add Another Page if needed.
  8. Tap Create PDF.
